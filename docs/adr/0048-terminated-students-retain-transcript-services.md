# Terminated Students retain historical Transcript services

**Status:** accepted — clarifies ADR-0040 for Records-owned document services

Termination blocks new academic mutations but preserves historical document services.
A Terminated Student may submit an Official Transcript Request and complete its payment,
cancellation, refund, and delivery workflow. Final termination leaves pending Transcript
Requests intact; Enrollment's academic-request closeout does not close Records requests.

Treating every Student-initiated write as an academic mutation would prevent obtaining
certified evidence of a retained academic record. Allowing these document-service writes
instead preserves that access without changing Grades, Enrollments, credit decisions,
Student Lifecycle Status, or enrollment rights. Ordinary Transcript authorization and fee
rules still apply.
