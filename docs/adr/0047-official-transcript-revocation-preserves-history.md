# Explicit Transcript revocation preserves issuance history

**Status:** accepted

Student Affairs may explicitly revoke an official Transcript issuance with a mandatory
reason. Records retains the immutable snapshot and records who revoked it and when;
revocation does not erase or edit the certified academic content. The revoked issuance's
public QR page shows only the issuance reference and Revoked status, withholding academic
details. The reason remains internal rather than appearing on that public page.

Issuing a corrected or newer Transcript does not automatically revoke earlier copies.
This separates a valid historical certification from an issuance Student Affairs has
explicitly withdrawn, while allowing disclosure through its public link to stop. Earlier
downloaded or printed copies cannot be recalled; their QR verification reports revocation.

Automatic invalidation on every later academic change was rejected because an issuance
certifies a dated snapshot rather than promising to remain the latest record. Keeping a
revoked issuance's full academic details public was rejected in favour of the reference
and status response. Internal issuance history, including revoked snapshots, is readable
by the owning Student, live Advisor, and Student Affairs, with the existing administrator
override, as recorded in SRS §4.9.
